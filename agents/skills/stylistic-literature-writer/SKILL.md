# Stylistic Literature Writer Skill

## Scope
Use this skill when drafting research articles, architectural decision docs, essays, newsletters, or stylistic copywriting.

## Guidelines
1. **Tone & Style Consistency**:
   - Establish a clear voice profile (e.g., authoritative, minimalist, academic, conversational) before drafting.
   - Maintain structural brevity: cut unnecessary jargon, passive phrasing, and adverbs.

2. **Structure & Logic Flow**:
   - Organize content sequentially with a clear thesis, supporting observations, and synthetically derived conclusions.
   - Build scannable layouts with hierarchical sectioning.

3. **Citations & Credibility**:
   - Ground factual claims with references or structured datasets.
   - Format source links, bibliographies, and inline citation styles uniformly.
